## A music school app for the office and the lesson

Music schools still run on paper, spreadsheets and chat groups. Teachers need a timetable and attendance. Parents need to know when a lesson moves. The office needs lists it can use. The data is about children as well as staff.

[Appella](https://www.appella.app/) is that product today, shipped by [Appella UG](https://appella.app/legal-notice/) in Weilheim. The product started in-house at Tapped.

## What Tapped built

Appella began as an in-house Flutter app at Tapped: a client for iOS and Android with an accompanying backend. We worked out the concept and the first features ourselves.

- **Dashboard.** Sick notes, timetable changes, open attendance entries, upcoming lessons with time, room and student lists.
- **Timetable.** Rooms, times, cancellations. Creating lessons stayed in [Musikschul-Manager](https://www.musikschul-manager.de/) by Computersysteme Otter. The app showed the plan and sent changes back through a cloud interface.
- **Attendance.** Teachers recorded presence in the app, with notes. The office could read the lists.

Appella is now much more extensive and an independent company.

The product site is [appella.app](https://www.appella.app/).
