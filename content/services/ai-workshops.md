Companies buy Copilot seats and then notice that nobody uses them on the real work. The tools do not know your backlog, your security rules, or the parts of the repo that must not be touched.

We run workshops for teams that are rolling out AI and need developers — and the rest of the team — to use it on their product. That means in the app, in the delivery workflow, and with coding agents (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Codex), under the constraints you already have: security, review, scale.

- "Code never compiles."
- "Coding is just half the meat."
- "Almost right, still wrong."
- "Reviews take longer, not shorter."
- "The seats sit idle. Nobody trusts the output."
- "The agent doesn't know our repo."
- "Code in the cloud, works council on the line."

## How a workshop runs

We look at the product, the team and how you work, and find where AI actually pays off. That means short surveys or interviews with people in the teams, and a clear view of what costs time and money. In the code we check whether the app can take AI features, whether agents can work in this repository without leaking secrets or producing diffs nobody can review, and where AI belongs in the workflow.

You do not get a long PDF of jargon and tool names. We implement the proposal with your team and change it if it does not hold.

Tools and models move fast. Telling useful from marketing is tiring. Once we know how you work, we tell you what is worth using.

## What you get

- Current state and target, with the engineering team
- The codebase: ready for AI in the app, in workflows, and for coding agents
- Which MCPs, tools and skills to start with
- Safeguards for security, review and scale

If you want a written review or audit rather than time with the team, that lives under [technical consulting]({{< relref "/services/technical-consulting.md" >}}).
