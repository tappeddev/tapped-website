We help you decide which technologies to choose when you build an app or a product. What drives cost, what slows you down, what makes you fast.

You talk to people who ship Flutter apps for a living, not a slide deck from someone who will not have to maintain the result.

## App audit

For products that already exist, we look at:

- The project and the code: structure, quality, what is expensive to change.
- The workflows: how the team ships, reviews, tests and releases.
- The integration into your systems: backend, identity, data, and everything the app has to talk to.
- AI integration: how AI is used in the product, and in the team and its workflows.

You get a written ranking of what to fix, what to leave, and what it costs to keep going as you are.

## Native to Flutter migration

Replacing two native apps is a product decision, not a framework demo. We start with what you already ship, then choose a path:

- Full replacement when core flows are changing anyway (the approach we took with MENTZ on [VVS Mobil]({{< relref "/work/vvs-mobil.md" >}})).
- Feature by feature when the surface area is large and the team still needs to learn Flutter.
- Stay native when Flutter would be a fashion choice, not a cheaper, safer codebase.
- Mix: Flutter components in a native app, or native components in a Flutter app.

A pilot in the real architecture beats a greenfield prototype that never has to talk to your backend.
