Tapped is an app and software development agency in Munich, focused on Flutter and modern cross-platform development.

Founded in Munich in 2019. The founders’ mobile experience goes back further: native iOS and Android apps well before the company, Flutter since the early beta. We work remotely from across Germany, and internationally.

From startups through mid-size companies to large enterprise projects, we build the apps. What that looks like in production is on our [projects]({{< relref "/work" >}}).
